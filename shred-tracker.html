🥩
Hit Protein First
At 185g, protein is your most critical lever. It preserves muscle while you cut and keeps you full. Build every meal around a protein anchor: eggs, chicken, beef, fish, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese.
⚖️
Weigh Yourself Weekly
Weigh every morning after using the bathroom, before eating. Daily weight fluctuates 2–4 lbs from water and food. What matters is the weekly average trend going down. Don't panic over day-to-day swings.
💧
Drink More Water
Aim for 1 gallon per day. Thirst is often mistaken for hunger. Drinking a full glass of water before meals reliably reduces calorie intake and helps with digestion.
😴
Sleep = Fat Loss
Poor sleep increases ghrelin (hunger hormone) and crushes fat oxidation. 7–9 hours is non-negotiable. Sleep deprivation at 40 also tanks testosterone, making muscle preservation harder.
🔄
Diet Breaks Work
Every 6–8 weeks at a deficit, eat at maintenance for 1–2 weeks. This resets leptin (fullness hormone) and prevents metabolic adaptation. You'll come back leaner and with more energy.
🏋️
Lift Heavy
Resistance training 3–4x/week is essential to preserve muscle on a cut. Don't just cardio your way thin — you'll lose muscle. Keep intensity high, adjust volume if recovery is slow.
